## 3. Definitions and statuses

- **Listing** — a user's post about a specific item or animal.
- **Lost** — the item belongs to the author of the listing and was lost by them.
- **Found** — the author found someone else's item and is looking for its owner.
- **Recovered** — closing status for a "Lost" listing: the item is back with its owner.
- **Returned to owner** — closing status for a "Found" listing: the item was handed over to its owner.
- **URGENT** — priority promotion of a listing, paid for with crystals.
- **Comment** — a public reply posted by a user under a listing.
- **Crystals** — internal in-app points (section 7).
- **Map** — the screen with pins marking where items were found or lost.
- **Profile** — the user's personal section: account name and crystal balance, listing management, terms and policy, feedback, log out and account deletion.

## 5. Publishing listings

5.1. A listing must match the selected status ("Lost" or "Found") and category and contain an accurate description that allows the owner to recognise their item: place, approximate time, distinguishing features, circumstances.

5.2. **Hidden-detail rule.** The published information must not be sufficient for a stranger to pass themselves off as the owner. Keep at least one significant detail private (an inscription, a scratch, the contents, a serial number) and use it as a verification question when handing over the item.

5.3. One item or one animal — one listing. Duplicates are removed.

5.4. Photos must show the item described. Other people's images, screenshots from the internet and photos you have no rights to are prohibited.

5.5. The **map pin** marks the place where the item was found or lost, not your home address. If the item was lost or found near your home, you may and should move the pin to the nearest public landmark.

5.6. A published listing can be edited: **Profile → My listings → Edit**. Editing is meant for clarifying details; replacing the content of a listing with a different item is prohibited — publish a new listing instead.

5.7. As soon as the item is found or handed over to its owner, the author must set the listing status to **"Recovered"** or **"Returned to owner"**.
